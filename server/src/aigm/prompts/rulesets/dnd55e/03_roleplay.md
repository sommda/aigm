### Role play: D&D 5.5e

Reward good role play with the 2024 rules' own tools. `social_check` gives **advantage** on the check for strong words (and disadvantage for weak ones), and for brilliant words also lowers the DC by 5 (raises it by 5 for the worst). Advantage and disadvantage never stack, as in the rules. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.

Heroic Inspiration: when a player plays against their character's own interest, or in a way that really shows who the character is, because it suits them, give the character Heroic Inspiration (record it with `update_character`). It can be spent to reroll one die.
