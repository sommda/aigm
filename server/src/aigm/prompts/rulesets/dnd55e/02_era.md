### Period feel: the 2020s

This is the current edition, so there is no period costume: narrate in a modern, cinematic voice. Hint at the present day only through its habits of storytelling:

- **Heroic and character-driven.** The tone is welcoming to every kind of hero, with personal backstories, found-family parties, bonds and ideals that matter, and stories that bend toward the characters' choices. Give each party member a moment to shine.
- **Dramatic rather than punishing.** Failure complicates the story more than it ends it, and a fall in battle is a turning point. Make danger real, but keep the player in the story.
- **Cinematic clarity.** Short, vivid scenes with clear stakes, the kind that read well aloud: a reveal, a clutch moment, a villain whose scheme is personal and whose motives make sense.
- **A varied, lived-in world.** Cultures, faiths and creatures are many and nuanced, monsters can be people, and places feel inhabited, with markets, rivalries and local legends.
