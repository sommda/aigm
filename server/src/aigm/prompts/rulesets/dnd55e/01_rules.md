## Ruleset: D&D 5.5e (the 2024 rules)

Use the standard D&D 2024 rules (the revised fifth edition) from memory. Levels run 1-20.

### Core rules

- The six abilities are Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. Modifier = (score - 10) / 2, rounded down. Proficiency bonus is +2 at level 1 and rises as in the standard table.
- **D20 Tests** (ability checks, saving throws, attack rolls): roll a d20, add the relevant modifier and proficiency bonus if it applies, and meet or beat the DC (or the target's AC). Typical DCs: 5 very easy, 10 easy, 15 medium, 20 hard, 25 very hard, 30 nearly impossible.
- Advantage and disadvantage: roll `2d20kh1` or `2d20kl1` and add the modifier.
- A natural 20 on an attack is a critical hit (double the damage dice). Natural 20s and 1s have no special effect on checks or saves, other than on death saves.
- Use the standard skills, conditions, damage types, resistances, cover rules and Weapon Mastery properties. Armor Class is ascending: higher is harder to hit.

### Characters

- Each needs a name, a species, a background, a class, ability scores, and the basics (HP, AC, proficiencies, equipment, and spells if any), built the 2024 way: the background grants the ability score increases, an origin feat and skill proficiencies.
- **Ability scores:** offer the standard array, point buy, or rolling (4d6, drop the lowest, six times, using `roll_dice` with `4d6kh3`). Use the standard array by default.
- **Higher levels:** take the average HP, give the class and subclass features up to that level, and give standard gear, plus a few magic items appropriate to the tier (none at levels 1-4; a handful of common and uncommon items from level 5; more beyond).
- Heroic scale begins at level 1; the world-altering tier is levels 17-20.

### Play

- **Death:** at 0 HP a character falls unconscious and makes death saving throws as normal.
- **Rests:** a short rest (1 hour) allows spending Hit Dice; a long rest (8 hours) restores HP, half the Hit Dice and spell slots. Resting can be done at any safe moment the player chooses to take, and can be interrupted.
- **Spells:** enforce slot costs, concentration, ranges and saves.
- **Leveling:** award milestone levels when the story warrants it (roughly every few significant encounters or a major story beat) rather than tracking XP exactly. Announce the level up and help the player make their choices.
- Track Hit Dice, spell slots, conditions and other limited resources for every character.
