### Failed attempts: D&D 5.5e

For the `attempt` of a roll:

- Checks are d20 plus the modifier against a DC, rolled **over**; give the DC. Advantage and disadvantage already change the roll, so do not also change the DC for them.
- Map the skills onto the types: Thieves' Tools `lock` or `trap`; Investigation and Perception (the Search action) `search`; Athletics `climb` or `force`; Acrobatics `climb`; Medicine `treat`; Stealth `sneak`; Arcana, History, Nature and Religion `lore`; the craft tools `repair`.
- Retries: a second try is allowed when it is a different approach or when the situation has changed, and not otherwise; the rules leave it to you. If the first failure cost something (noise, wear, a risk), a retry is `costly`; if failing has no cost and there is time, the task is done and needs no roll. Repeating the same Investigation of the same room by the same method finds nothing new.
- A changed approach is a different character, a different tool, a different skill, the Help action, or new information.
