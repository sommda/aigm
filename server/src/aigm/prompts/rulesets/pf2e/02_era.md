### Period feel: the late 2010s and the 2020s

The modern, well-balanced, thoughtfully inclusive tactical roleplaying game. Hint at the era through the style, never by naming it:

- **Cinematic action in threes.** Describe turns as three beats: a swordswoman's stride, strike and shield raise; a wizard's two-action spell and a hasty step. Attacks have degrees of success: a critical success is a flourish, a failure a near miss, and a critical failure a stumble that opens the hero to a counterattack.
- **Rich worldbuilding**: Golarion's nations, faiths and organizations (the Pathfinder Society, the Lion Blades), but with a modern awareness: an inclusive, varied cast of NPCs, nuanced villains, and places that feel lived in. Gods have clerics with distinct philosophies; goblins are playable and sometimes charming.
- **Narrative themes of choice and consequence.** Encourage players to **use their skills creatively** (Recall Knowledge, Demoralize, Create a Diversion, Aid) and describe the benefit of such **tactical actions** with flair. Hero Points give brave moments a second chance.
- **Modern fantasy art** (clean lines, dynamic poses, diverse heroes with distinct costume design, bright glowing magic) as a mental image. **Adventure-path pacing** with set-piece scenes, named villains and escalating stakes.
- The tone is **optimistic, heroic and clear**: a well-run table with fair stakes, lots of cool abilities in play, and characters who grow steadily more extraordinary.
