## Ruleset: Pathfinder, 2nd Edition (Remaster)

Use the Pathfinder 2nd Edition rules, in their Remaster form (Player Core, GM Core, Monster Core, 2023), from memory. Levels run 1-20. It is a ground-up redesign: a unified proficiency system, a three-action economy, degrees of success and tightly balanced encounters. The default setting is the world of Golarion, but a custom setting is fine.

### Characters

- **Build:** a character has an **ancestry** (with a **heritage** and ancestry feats), a **background**, a **class** and **attributes**: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, which are stored as modifiers. Every attribute starts at +0, then gains +1 boosts from the ancestry, the background (two choices), the class's key attribute, and four free boosts; no attribute starts above +4.
- **Classes:** Alchemist, Barbarian, Bard, Champion, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Investigator, Monk, Oracle, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Swashbuckler, Witch, Wizard (and more in later books). Each has class feats at every level, ancestry feats, skill feats and general feats.
- **Proficiency:** untrained, trained, expert, master, legendary: a statistic's modifier is the attribute + the proficiency (+2, +4, +6, +8 for trained to legendary, plus the character's level if trained or better, nothing if untrained).
- **Hit points:** ancestry HP + (class HP + Constitution modifier) x level. **Armor Class** = 10 + Dexterity (capped by armor) + armor bonus + proficiency (with level). **Higher AC is better.**
- **Level up:** 1,000 XP per level (a milestone is fine here); each level brings a set of choices.

### Play

- **Degrees of success:** a check is `1d20` + modifier vs a DC. Beat the DC by 10 or more: critical success; meet or beat: success; fail: failure; fail by 10 or more: critical failure. A natural 20 improves the degree by one step, and a natural 1 worsens it by one. Apply this to attacks, saves and skill checks.
- **Actions:** each turn, three actions and one reaction. Strike, Stride, Raise a Shield, Interact and so on are single actions; spells often cost two. Activities take multiple actions. **Multiple Attack Penalty:** -5 on the second attack and -10 on later ones in a turn (-4 and -8 with agile weapons).
- **Initiative:** use Perception (or another skill by circumstance), highest first. Saves: Fortitude, Reflex, Will.
- **Conditions** matter: frightened, off-guard (formerly flat-footed), prone, grabbed, stunned, slowed and so on, each with a defined effect.
- **Death:** at 0 HP a character is unconscious and **dying 1** (a critical hit puts them at dying 2). Each turn they make a recovery flat check (DC 10 + dying value); the dying value reaches 4 means death. Healing wakes them and raises their **wounded** value. **Hero Points** (earned by the party) can reroll or save a character from death.
- **Rest and healing:** a night's rest (8 hours) restores HP and daily preparations; **Treat Wounds** (Medicine, 10 minutes) heals between fights.
- **Magic:** four traditions (arcane, divine, occult, primal); spell ranks 1-10, cantrips; prepared and spontaneous casters; focus points for focus spells. Heightening a spell makes it more powerful.
- **Challenge:** creatures have a level; an XP budget by encounter severity (trivial, low, moderate, severe, extreme) guides fights. A party-level moderate encounter is a fair fight. Monsters are tough and the proficiency system makes enemies several levels above the party deadly.
