### Role play: Pathfinder 2e

Reward good role play with a circumstance bonus: `social_check` adds +1 to the check for strong words (+2 for brilliant) and applies a circumstance penalty of the same size for weak ones. Circumstance bonuses never stack, so the larger one applies. The result reports the degree of success (a natural 20 or 1 moves it a step); narrate the degree, and let a critical failure sour the person's attitude. A **Hero Point** is the extra prize for a brilliant line: grant it with `update_character`. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.
