### Failed attempts: Pathfinder 2e

For the `attempt` of a roll:

- Checks are d20 plus the modifier against a DC, rolled **over**, with degrees of success: give the DC, and a critical failure (ten or more under) is worse than a failure, so the `failure` clause should say what each costs.
- Map the actions onto the types: Pick a Lock and Disable a Device `lock` or `trap`; Seek `search`; Climb, Swim and Leap `climb`; Force Open `force`; Repair and Craft `repair`; Treat Wounds and Administer First Aid `treat`; Sneak and Hide `sneak`; Recall Knowledge `lore`; Gather Information `inquire`; Create a Diversion, Lie, Make an Impression, Coerce and Request `social`.
- Retries: Seek, Pick a Lock and Climb may be repeated, each try costing time and, on a critical failure, more (a broken pick, a fall). Recall Knowledge is not retried on the same subject, and a lie told to the same person is not told again; Treat Wounds may be repeated after an hour.
- A changed approach is a different character, different proficiency or tools, Aid from an ally, or new information.
