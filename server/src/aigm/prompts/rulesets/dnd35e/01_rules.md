## Ruleset: D&D 3.5 Edition

Use the 3.5 Edition rules (revised Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide and Monster Manual, 2003) from memory, with the supplements' options when asked. Levels run 1-20 (epic beyond). It is the 3rd Edition d20 System, tidied: use the 3.5 versions of everything.

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. Roll `4d6kh3` six times (assigned freely), or use the 25-point buy. Modifier = (score - 10) / 2, rounded down.
- **Races:** Human, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Half-elf, Half-orc, Halfling, each with ability adjustments and traits. No favored class penalties apply to humans and half-elves; others use a favored class for multiclassing.
- **Classes:** Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Wizard (and the Complete books' classes and prestige classes when asked). Rangers, monks and paladins and their spells are redesigned from 3.0.
- **Skills and feats:** skill points x4 at first level (4 + Intelligence modifier, times 4), then per level; ranks cap at level + 3 (class skills). A feat at 1st level, then at 3rd, 6th and so on. Skill check = `1d20` + ranks + ability modifier + other vs a DC. **Hit points:** maximum at first level, then the class die plus Constitution.
- **Armor Class is ascending:** 10 + armor + shield + Dexterity + size + natural + deflection and other bonuses. Higher is better.

### Play

- **Attack:** `1d20` + base attack bonus + ability modifier + other vs AC. A natural 20 threatens a critical (confirm with a second attack roll; the weapon sets the multiplier and threat range); a natural 1 misses.
- **Initiative:** `1d20` + Dexterity modifier. Rounds are 6 seconds; a standard action plus a move action (or a full-round action) per round. Attacks of opportunity, flanking (+2), grapple, trip, bull rush and the like use the standard combat maneuvers.
- **Saving throws:** Fortitude, Reflex, Will: `1d20` + base save + ability modifier vs a DC.
- **Death:** 0 HP is disabled (one standard action at a time), -1 to -9 is dying (10% each round to stabilize), -10 is dead.
- **Magic:** wizards prepare from a spellbook; clerics and druids prepare from their lists; sorcerers and bards cast spontaneously. Spell levels 0-9, concentration checks, spell resistance and metamagic. Arcane spell failure by armor.
- **Challenge:** Challenge Ratings and Encounter Levels set difficulty. The wealth-by-level table sets magic item expectations, and the 3.5 magic item prices and crafting rules apply.
- **Advancement:** XP by CR and the party's level; milestone leveling is fine here. Multiclass characters and prestige classes (Arcane Trickster, Eldritch Knight, Dragon Disciple, Shadowdancer) come in the middle levels.
