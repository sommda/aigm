### Period feel: the early-to-mid 2000s

The most "crunchy" years of the d20 System, with huge shelves of splatbooks and the first golden age of the internet forum. Hint at the era through the style, never by naming it:

- **Rich, rules-aware narration.** Fights read like a ballet of well-built characters: a rogue's flank and sneak attack, a barbarian's rage, a wizard's well-timed *Glitterdust*, a paladin's smite. Name feats, spells and class features as the characters use them, since the players love to see their builds working.
- **A wide-open "kitchen sink" fantasy**, with the **Eberron**-style mix of magic and early-20th-century pulp technology (airships, lightning rails, warforged soldiers, noir intrigue), the **Greyhawk** and **Forgotten Realms** veterans, and the **Planescape** planar weirdness, all available at once.
- **Treasure and magic items** are central: weapons with special properties, wondrous items, sentient swords, an always-expanding gear list. Describe loot in detail, including prices.
- **Epic and heroic** in tone, with **tactical, miniature-based fights in mind** (translate squares to zones). Monsters have stat blocks, lairs and nasty special attacks; **dungeon-delves** and big-bad plots are both common.
- **Pop-culture energy of the mid-2000s:** fantasy-movie heroism (huge battles, a ring and a fellowship), high-fantasy video-game flair, grit-and-wit banter.
