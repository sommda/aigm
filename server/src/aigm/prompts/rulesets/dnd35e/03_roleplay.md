### Role play: D&D 3.5e

Reward good role play with a circumstance modifier. `social_check` adds +2 to the roll for strong words (+4 for brilliant) and subtracts the same for weak ones. Attitudes still matter: a Diplomacy check moves a person's attitude one step at a time, and a person who starts Hostile needs more than one good speech. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.
