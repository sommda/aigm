## Ruleset: D&D BECMI (the Mentzer "red box" line and Rules Cyclopedia)

Use the BECMI rules, with the Rules Cyclopedia's General Skills and Weapon Mastery as options. Levels run 1-36. The line is a refinement of the B/X rules, with an escalating scale: dungeon crawling at levels 1-14, domain rulership around levels 9-14, and world-shaking Companion and Master play beyond. The default setting is the Known World (Mystara).

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Constitution, Charisma. Roll `3d6` in order (with the usual 2-for-1 trade into the prime requisite). Modifiers range from -3 to +3.
- **Classes:** Cleric, Fighter, Magic-User, Thief, Dwarf, Elf, Halfling (demihumans are classes with level caps, 12, 10 and 8 in the Rules Cyclopedia, beyond which they advance by "attack ranks"). The Rules Cyclopedia adds the human Avenger, Druid, Knight, Paladin and Mystic. Alignment is Lawful, Neutral or Chaotic.
- **Hit points:** as in B/X, with fixed gains after the name level (around 9th). The Constitution modifier applies.
- **Armor Class is descending**, as in B/X (9 unarmored, leather 7, chain 5, plate 3, a shield improves it by 1). **Lower is better.** **Skills:** under the Rules Cyclopedia, each character has a few General Skills tied to an ability (Riding, Swimming, Blacksmithing, Survival, and so on), checked on `1d20` at or under the ability score. **Weapon Mastery:** fighters become skilled, expert, master or grand master of a weapon, improving damage and special effects.

### Play

- **To hit:** the target number is THAC0 minus the target's AC. THAC0 for a 1st-level fighter is 19; it improves with level (and by rank up to level 36). A natural 20 always hits and a 1 always misses.
- **Initiative:** `1d6` per side, higher goes first. Surprise `1d6`, surprised on a 1-2. Morale `2d6`. Reaction `2d6`.
- **Saving throws:** five categories as in B/X, rolled on `1d20`.
- **Death:** 0 HP is death. High-level clerics can raise the dead; the Immortal's quest is the final stage of the game.
- **Magic:** Vancian memorization. Magic-users and clerics gain 1st-level spells; elves cast as magic-users. High-level spells and magic items are common in the Companion and Master range.
- **Advancement:** XP from monsters and treasure (1 gp = 1 XP); level titles are flavor. The game's later levels bring strongholds, armies, domains and planar travel. Treat these as story hooks: a 9th-level hero builds a keep and rules the land around it.
- **Danger:** the early levels are as deadly as B/X's, and the later levels trade danger for scope.
