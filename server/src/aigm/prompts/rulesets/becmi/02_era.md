### Period feel: the mid-1980s

The era of the red box, Saturday-morning cartoons and the great expansions. Hint at it through the style, never by naming it:

- A **bright, friendly, heroic fantasy**: a clear moral shape, with noble knights, helpful wise wizards, cheerful dwarf miners and treacherous goblin lords. The tone is that of a **cartoon adventure** with real swords: a swashbuckling party, a mysterious guide, a tough foe and a daring escape.
- **Worlds you can name**: kingdoms with colorful names and flags, gazetteers of places like Darokin, Karameikos, the Glantri wizard-lands, and the Savage Coast. Describe borders, rulers, guilds and heraldic colors. The Known World is flat and lived-in, a patchwork of "Mystara"-like peoples and modern-day-Earth echoes.
- **Narration with narrator warmth**: a storyteller's voice that addresses the party directly ("Before you stands...") and sometimes comments gently on what is happening. Dungeons are real, but the world beyond them is the point: roads, ports, caravans, strongholds, tournaments, ancient prophecies.
- **Scale grows**: heroes who start in a cave in a canyon end up rulers of dominions with armies, travelers between worlds, and at the end, candidates for immortality. Let the player feel that growth, with castles to build and titles to earn.
- Treasure is gold, gems and magic items with names. Monsters often speak, bargain and keep promises.
