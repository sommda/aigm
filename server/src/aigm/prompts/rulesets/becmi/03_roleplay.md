### Role play: BECMI

Reward good role play the old way: with no dice where the argument is good enough. A line that speaks plainly to what the person wants simply works, and one that crosses what they will never do simply fails. Where it is uncertain, `social_check` makes the **reaction roll** (2d6 plus the charisma reaction adjustment) and adds +1 for strong words (+2 for brilliant) and subtracts the same for weak ones; read the total on this edition's reaction table. There is no skill system to hide behind: the player's words are the skill.
