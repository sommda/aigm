## Ruleset: D&D Basic (Moldvay/Cook, "B/X")

Use the 1981 Basic and Expert rule sets. Levels run 1-14. The rules are clear, compact and lethal. Demihumans are classes of their own.

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Constitution, Charisma. Roll `3d6` for each, in order. A character may lower Strength, Intelligence or Wisdom by 2 to raise the prime requisite by 1 (never below 9; Dexterity, Constitution and Charisma may not be traded). Modifiers range -3 to +3 only at the extremes.
- **Classes:** Cleric, Fighter, Magic-User, Thief; and the demihuman classes Dwarf, Elf (fighter and magic-user at once) and Halfling, each with a level cap. Alignment is Lawful, Neutral or Chaotic. A prime requisite of 13+ gives +5% XP, and 16+ gives +10%.
- **Hit points:** roll the class's die at first level (cleric d6, dwarf d8, elf d6, fighter d8, halfling d6, magic-user d4, thief d4) and add the Constitution modifier (minimum 1 per die). Allow a reroll of 1s and 2s at first level.
- **Armor Class is descending:** 9 unarmored, leather 7, chain 5, plate 3, a shield improves it by 1. **Lower is better.** Gear is bought with `3d6 x 10` gold pieces.
- **Gear:** equipment lists matter: torches or lanterns, rope, iron spikes, rations and a 10-foot pole are all part of surviving a dungeon.

### Play

- **To hit:** attack matrix equivalent: target number = THAC0 minus the target's AC, and roll `1d20` for that number or higher. Every first-level character has THAC0 19 (so needs 10 or better against AC 9, and 19 against AC 0), improving with level: fighters fastest, clerics and thieves slower, magic-users slowest. A natural 20 always hits and 1 always misses.
- **Initiative:** each side rolls `1d6` and the higher goes first. Surprise: `1d6`, surprised on a 1-2.
- **Saving throws:** Death Ray or Poison, Magic Wands, Paralysis or Turn to Stone, Dragon Breath, Rods/Staves/Spells. Roll `1d20` and meet or beat the class's number; it falls with level.
- **Death:** 0 HP is death. Only raise-dead magic recovers a character.
- **Magic:** spells are memorized by level and forgotten when cast. A first-level magic-user has one spell. Spell books are precious. Clerics cast no spells at 1st level.
- **Exploration:** ten-minute turns; check for wandering monsters every couple of turns (1 in 6), listening at doors (1 in 6; 2 in 6 for demihumans), searching for secret doors (1 in 6; 2 in 6 for elves). The Thief's skills are percentile (`1d100`, success at or under the number). Reaction rolls `2d6`; Morale `2d6`. Light sources burn out; track them when the story calls for it.
- **Advancement:** XP from monsters and treasure (1 gp = 1 XP). Level titles like Veteran, Magician, Acolyte, Thief's name are part of the flavor. Retainers can be hired, with loyalty and morale.
- **Danger:** with 4 to 8 hit points and no dying phase, a single bad fight can end a character. Retreat, rest, scout and hire help.
