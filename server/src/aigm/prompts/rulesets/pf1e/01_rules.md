## Ruleset: Pathfinder, 1st Edition

Use the Pathfinder 1st Edition rules (Core Rulebook, Bestiary and the common supplements) from memory. Levels run 1-20. It is the 3.5 Edition d20 System, extended and rebalanced: more options per class, more power for the martial classes, and hundreds of archetypes, feats and spells. The default setting is the world of Golarion, but a custom setting is fine.

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. Use the point buy (15 points for a standard game, 20 for high fantasy, 25 for epic), or `4d6kh3` six times, assigned freely. Modifier = (score - 10) / 2, rounded down.
- **Races:** Human, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Half-elf, Half-orc, Halfling (core), with racial ability adjustments and traits (and many more in the Advanced Race Guide).
- **Classes:** Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Wizard (core), plus the APG's Alchemist, Cavalier, Gunslinger, Inquisitor, Magus, Oracle, Summoner, Witch and others. Each class has class features, and many offer archetypes. Characters gain a favored class bonus (+1 HP or +1 skill rank per level).
- **Skills and feats:** skill ranks per level by class and Intelligence; a class skill with at least one rank gains +3. Characters choose **two traits** at creation. A feat at 1st level and every 2 levels. Skill check = `1d20` + ranks + ability modifier + other vs a DC.
- **Hit points:** maximum at first level, then average (or roll) per level, with Constitution added per level.
- **Armor Class is ascending:** 10 + armor + shield + Dexterity + size + natural + deflection and others. **Combat Maneuver Bonus/Defense** handle grapple, trip and so on.

### Play

- **Attack:** `1d20` + base attack bonus + ability modifier + other vs AC. Natural 20 threatens a critical (confirm with a second roll); natural 1 misses.
- **Initiative:** `1d20` + Dexterity modifier. A round is 6 seconds; a standard and a move action (or a full-round action) per round. Attacks of opportunity and flanking (+2) apply.
- **Saving throws:** Fortitude, Reflex, Will: `1d20` + base save + ability modifier vs a DC.
- **Death:** disabled at 0 HP; dying at -1 to your negative Constitution score; dead at negative Constitution. A dying character rolls to stabilize each round.
- **Magic:** wizards prepare from a spellbook; clerics and druids prepare from their lists; sorcerers, bards, oracles and summoners cast spontaneously. Spell levels 0-9, cantrips are unlimited. Concentration checks, spell resistance and metamagic apply.
- **Challenge:** Challenge Ratings and the Medium advancement track (2,000 XP for 2nd level); magic items are expected by the wealth-by-level table. Milestone leveling is fine here.
