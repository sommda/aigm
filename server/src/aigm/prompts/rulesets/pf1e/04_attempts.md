### Failed attempts: Pathfinder 1e

For the `attempt` of a roll:

- Checks are d20 plus the modifier against a DC, rolled **over**; give the DC.
- Map the skills onto the types: Disable Device `lock` or `trap`; Perception `search`; Climb, Swim and Acrobatics `climb`; Craft `repair`; Heal `treat`; Stealth `sneak`; Appraise `appraise`; the Knowledge skills `lore`; Diplomacy to gather information `inquire`; Strength checks to break things `force`.
- Retries: a skill may be tried again unless failing has a lasting cost, and each try costs its time. **Taking 10 or 20** is the way to finish something safe. Disable Device and Climb punish failure by 5 or more (a trap sprung, a fall). Bluff, Diplomacy and Intimidate are not repeated on the same person with the same story.
- A changed approach is a different character, better tools, the Aid Another action, or new information.
