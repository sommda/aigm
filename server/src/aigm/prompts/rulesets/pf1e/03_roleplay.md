### Role play: Pathfinder 1e

Reward good role play with a circumstance bonus. `social_check` adds +2 to the roll for strong words (+4 for brilliant) and subtracts the same for weak ones. A person's attitude (Hostile to Helpful) sets how hard they are to move. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.
