### Role play: D&D 4e

Reward good role play with a bonus. `social_check` adds +2 to the check for strong words (+4 for brilliant) and subtracts the same for weak ones. In a skill challenge, each person who speaks well is worth a success they could not have had by rolling alone. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.
