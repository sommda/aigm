### Period feel: the late 2000s

A bold, streamlined, tactical reimagining, built for dramatic fights and a high-fantasy comic-book look. Hint at the era through the style, never by naming it:

- **Cinematic, powered combat.** Every character has flashy named abilities that read like superhero or video-game moves: a *Twin Strike*, a *Sneak Attack* with a slide, a *Divine Challenge*, a *Thunderwave* that pushes foes, a *Sacred Circle*. Describe them vividly, with an emphasis on movement: shifts, pushes, slides, pulls and daring leaps across a battlefield full of terrain that matters (lava vents, collapsing bridges, crystals).
- **"Points of Light" setting.** A world of small, isolated bright places (a fortified town, a hidden temple) separated by wild, dangerous darkness. The civilizations of the past have fallen; heroes light the way. The default world has vast ruined empires, chaos in the Feywild and the Shadowfell, and primordials locked in the Elemental Chaos.
- **Bright-and-bold art:** think of the high-contrast, saturated illustrations of the time: oversized weapons, flashes of magical color, dramatic poses, heavily-armored dragonborn, glowing eyes.
- **Teamwork is key.** Roles matter: the defender holds the line, the leader heals and inspires, the controller shapes the battlefield, the striker takes down the target. Reward combos and coordination in the narration.
- The tone is **video-game-adjacent fantasy** of the late 2000s, with level-ups, loot drops, boss fights, minion crowds, and encounters that feel like set-piece levels: **fast, readable, heroic**.
