### Failed attempts: D&D 4e

For the `attempt` of a roll:

- Checks are d20 plus the modifier against a DC, rolled **over**; give the DC.
- Map the skills onto the types: Thievery `lock` or `trap`; Perception and Dungeoneering `search`; Athletics `climb` or `force`; Endurance and Acrobatics `climb`; Heal `treat`; Stealth `sneak`; Arcana, History, Nature and Religion `lore`; Streetwise `inquire`; Bluff, Diplomacy and Intimidate `social`.
- Retries: a skill check may be tried again unless failure has a consequence that makes the second try pointless or the circumstances have not changed; a retry costs its time. In a skill challenge, each failure counts toward the limit and is a cost of its own.
- A changed approach is a different skill, a different character, a different tool, or new information.
