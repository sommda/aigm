## Ruleset: D&D 3rd Edition

Use the 3rd Edition rules (Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual, 2000) from memory. Levels run 1-20 (epic play beyond, rarely). It is the d20 System: one core mechanic, a thick layer of customization (feats, skills, prestige classes), and a rule for nearly everything.

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. Roll `4d6kh3` six times (assigned freely), or use the point-buy variant. Modifier = (score - 10) / 2, rounded down.
- **Races:** Human, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Half-elf, Half-orc, Halfling, each with ability adjustments, a favored class and special traits.
- **Classes:** Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Wizard. Multiclassing is free but penalizes experience if classes differ too much from the favored class. Prestige classes (Arcane Archer, Assassin, Blackguard, and so on) are entered later.
- **Skills and feats:** skill points per level by class and Intelligence; ranks cap at level + 3 (class skills) or half that (cross-class). Characters get a feat at 1st level and every 3 levels (fighters get bonus feats). Skill checks are `1d20` + ranks + ability modifier + other bonuses vs a DC.
- **Hit points:** maximum at first level, then the class die each level plus Constitution (fighter d10, barbarian d12, rogue d6, wizard d4 and so on).
- **Armor Class is ascending:** 10 + armor bonus + shield bonus + Dexterity modifier + size + other. Higher is better.
- **Starting gear:** by class's starting gold; the Dungeon Master's Guide has a wealth-by-level table for starting above level 1.

### Play

- **Attack:** `1d20` + base attack bonus + Strength (melee) or Dexterity (ranged) modifier + other bonuses vs the target's AC. Natural 20 threatens a critical (a confirming roll doubles or triples damage by weapon); natural 1 misses.
- **Initiative:** `1d20` + Dexterity modifier. Combat has a round of 6 seconds: a standard action and a move action, or a full-round action. **Attacks of opportunity** punish moving past or casting near foes; flanking gives +2.
- **Saving throws:** Fortitude, Reflex and Will, `1d20` + class base save + ability modifier vs a DC (spell DC = 10 + spell level + casting ability modifier).
- **Death:** 0 HP is disabled; -1 to -9 is dying (a stabilization roll each round, 10% chance); -10 is dead. Healing brings a character back up.
- **Magic:** wizards prepare spells from a spellbook, clerics and druids prepare from their full list; sorcerers and bards cast spontaneously from spells known. Spell levels 0-9, spell resistance, concentration checks and metamagic feats.
- **Challenge:** monsters have a Challenge Rating (CR); an encounter's Encounter Level (EL) sets its difficulty. An EL equal to the party's level is a fair fight, two or three above is dangerous, and four above can be deadly. Magic items are expected: characters of a given level should own about a standard amount of wealth.
- **Advancement:** XP from defeating monsters and overcoming challenges, divided by party size; 1,000 XP x (level - 1) x level / 2 to reach each level. Milestone leveling is fine for this digital game.
