### Role play: D&D 3e

Reward good role play with a circumstance modifier. `social_check` adds +2 to the roll for strong words (+4 for brilliant) and subtracts the same for weak ones. Attitudes still matter: a person who starts Hostile or Unfriendly needs more than one good speech. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.
