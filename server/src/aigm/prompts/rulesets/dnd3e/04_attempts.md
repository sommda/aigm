### Failed attempts: D&D 3e

For the `attempt` of a roll:

- Checks are d20 plus the modifier against a DC, rolled **over**; give the DC.
- Map the skills onto the types: Open Lock and Disable Device `lock` or `trap`; Search `search`; Climb, Swim and Jump `climb`; Craft and Repair `repair`; Heal `treat`; Move Silently and Hide `sneak`; Appraise `appraise`; Knowledge `lore`; Gather Information `inquire`; Strength checks to break things `force`.
- Retries: a skill may be tried again unless the rules say otherwise or failing has a lasting cost, and then each try costs its time. **Taking 20** (twenty times the time, with no penalty for failure) is the way to finish something safe: a search of a room, a lock with no one watching. Disable Device and Climb punish failure by 5 or more (a trap sprung, a fall), so a failure that bad is costly in its own right. Bluff, Diplomacy and Intimidate are not repeated on the same person with the same story.
- A changed approach is a different character, better tools (masterwork thieves' tools give a bonus), help from another character, or new information.
