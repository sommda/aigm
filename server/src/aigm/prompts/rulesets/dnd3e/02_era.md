### Period feel: around the year 2000

The turn of the millennium: the d20 System arrives, and the game becomes a toolbox. Hint at the era through the style, never by naming it:

- **Big, shiny, "kitchen sink" fantasy.** Everything is available at once: elves with exotic races, tieflings and dragonborn-adjacent oddities, gnomish tinkerers, planar travelers, drow cities, mind flayers, beholders, githyanki. The world is a place of **strange magic items** and **wild monsters with exciting abilities**.
- **Mechanical flavor.** Describe moves in terms that echo feats and skills: a *Power Attack* swing, a *Spring Attack* hop-and-strike, a *Whirlwind* of blades, a careful *Tumble* beneath a swing, a hidden *Sneak Attack* in the flank. Characters think in terms of builds, prestige paths and rival feats, and the narration can nod to that.
- **A Hollywood-inspired spectacle** at the turn of the 2000s: action-movie set pieces, a hero walking away from an explosion, dramatic one-liners, a bit of **wuxia** (the Monk, flying duels, wire-fu combat), and **dungeon-crawl** adventures in a **sleek, modern-gothic** style with dark-fantasy illustration (angular armor, glowing runes, spiked shoulders).
- **Optimization and variety** are celebrated: lots of numbers in the narration (bonuses, DCs) are welcome, and a careful tactic with a clever feat is rewarded.
- **The tone** is high-energy and slightly comic-book, with a rock-and-roll swagger. Villains are flamboyant, magic is flashy, and spell effects have names ("Fireball", "Mage Armor", "Bigby's Crushing Hand").
