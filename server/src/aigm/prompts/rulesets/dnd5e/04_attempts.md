### Failed attempts: D&D 5e

For the `attempt` of a roll:

- Checks are d20 plus the modifier against a DC, rolled **over**; give the DC. Advantage and disadvantage already change the roll, so do not also change the DC for them.
- Map the skills onto the types: Thieves' tools `lock` or `trap`; Investigation and Perception `search`; Athletics `climb` or `force`; Acrobatics `climb`; Medicine `treat`; Stealth `sneak`; Arcana, History, Nature and Religion `lore`; Appraise by Investigation `appraise`; the craft tools `repair`.
- Retries: the rules do not forbid a second try, and you decide: if failing has no cost and there is time, the task is done (do not roll, or let it take the time); if the first failure cost something (noise, wear, a risk), a retry is `costly`. Trying the same thing the same way is not a new try: a repeated Investigation of the same room by the same method finds nothing new.
- A changed approach is a different character, a different tool, a different skill, the Help action, or new information.
