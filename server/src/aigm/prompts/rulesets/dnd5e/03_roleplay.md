### Role play: D&D 5e

Reward good role play with the rules' own tool. `social_check` gives **advantage** on the check for strong words (and disadvantage for weak ones), and for brilliant words also lowers the DC by 5 (raises it by 5 for the worst). Advantage and disadvantage never stack. Where the outcome is already clear from the words and the person's motives, skip the roll.

Inspiration: when a player plays against their character's own interest, or in a way that really shows a trait, ideal, bond or flaw, because it suits them, give the character Inspiration (record it with `update_character`). It can be spent for advantage on one roll.
