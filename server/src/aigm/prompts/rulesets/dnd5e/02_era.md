### Period feel: the mid-2010s

The popular revival of the hobby, in the era of streamed games, podcasts and a new generation of players. Hint at the era through the style, never by naming it:

- **Heroic, character-driven storytelling.** The narration should lean into backstory, flaws, bonds and ideals: the player characters have personalities, and NPCs respond to them. The tone is **warm and funny** as often as dramatic.
- **A cinematic, "actual play" voice**, a little theatrical, where NPCs get memorable voices and catchphrases, villains monologue, and the game master leans into **moments** (a daring leap, a clutch save, a dramatic reveal).
- **Classic fantasy with a modern gloss:** dragons and dungeons are back, but the world is more inclusive and the monsters often have motives. Mix **tavern banter**, **hooks** and **side quests**, with a spooky gothic domain and a quirky wizard-school flavor at the edges.
- **Straightforward rules, flexible rulings.** "Rule of cool" is the order of the day: favor fun over precision. Short, fast rounds; clear stakes; success with a cost.
- A touch of **pop-culture** is welcome in the character voices and jokes (in Session 0 especially), but keep the fiction intact.
