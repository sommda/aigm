## Ruleset: D&D 5th Edition (2014)

Use the 2014 fifth-edition rules (Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual) from memory. Levels run 1-20. This is the immediate predecessor of the 2024 revision; use the 2014 version of everything (not the 2024 changes).

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. Modifier = (score - 10) / 2, rounded down. Use the standard array (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8), point buy (27 points) or `4d6kh3` six times. **Species ("race") grants ability score increases** (and subraces); backgrounds grant skills, tools, languages and a feature, but no ability increases.
- **Characters** need a race, a class (with subclass at level 1-3), a background, ability scores, HP, AC, proficiencies, equipment and spells. Feats are optional (taken in place of an ability score improvement). Multiclassing is allowed.
- Proficiency bonus is +2 at level 1 and rises as in the table.

### Play

- **D20 Tests:** roll a d20 + modifier + proficiency if it applies, against a DC or AC. Typical DCs: 5 very easy, 10 easy, 15 medium, 20 hard, 25 very hard, 30 nearly impossible. Advantage and disadvantage: `2d20kh1` or `2d20kl1`.
- A natural 20 on an attack is a critical hit (double the damage dice); natural 20s and 1s do nothing special on checks and saves, except that a natural 20 on a death save restores 1 HP.
- **Initiative:** Dexterity check, highest first. **Armor Class is ascending.** Attacks of opportunity apply; there is no Weapon Mastery.
- **Death:** at 0 HP a character falls unconscious and makes death saves (three successes stabilize, three failures kill; massive damage can kill outright).
- **Rests:** a short rest (1 hour) allows spending Hit Dice; a long rest (8 hours) restores all HP, half the Hit Dice and spell slots.
- **Spells:** slots, concentration, components, rituals and ranges as in the 2014 book; prepared casters (cleric, druid, wizard) choose daily.
- **Advancement:** XP or milestone; announce level-ups and help with the choices. Inspiration rewards roleplay.
