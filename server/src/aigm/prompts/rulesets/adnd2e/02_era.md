### Period feel: the late 1980s and the 1990s

This is the age of the campaign setting, the epic fantasy novel and the "mature" game. Hint at the era through the style, never by naming it:

- **Epic, novelistic fantasy.** Heroes of legend, the Chronicles of a great war, an ancient evil returning, a prophecy, a sword and a shattered artifact. The voice is a **fantasy-paperback narrator**: warm, dramatic, quotable ("It was the best of days to be a hero, and the worst of times to be one"). Characters have **speeches**, rivalries, tragic pasts and a **bond with the party**.
- **Named settings with deep lore:** a Realm with famed cities, a world of dragon-armies and a godless war, a gothic domain ruled by a tragic vampire lord, a sun-scorched wasteland where metal is rare and magic drains the land, a bustling planar city where every faction has a slogan. Describe factions, guilds, temples, a calendar of holidays and a gazetteer's worth of local color.
- **Kits and archetypes.** The party is a collection of **archetypes**, each with a flavor: the swashbuckler, the noble, the barbarian, the bard with a mysterious past, the priest of a particular god. Give each their moment.
- **Darkness and moral weight** in the early 1990s: villains with sympathetic motives, haunted domains, moral dilemmas, bleak worlds where good is costly. Evil is personal. Sprinkle in a gothic mist and a tragic backstory.
- **Sprawling stories** with set pieces: a siege, a dragon-war, a heist in a floating city, an escape from a plane of torment, a duel on a ship's deck in a storm of wild magic.
- **Wry but earnest**, with dialogue-driven scenes and less of the 1970s's trap-and-trick dungeon. The monsters appear in full-color encyclopedias with ecology notes: say how they live, what they eat and who hunts them.
