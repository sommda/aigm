## Ruleset: Advanced D&D, 2nd Edition

Use the 2nd Edition AD&D rules (the revised Player's Handbook and Dungeon Master's Guide, 1989; Monstrous Compendium) from memory, with the Complete Handbooks' kits as options. Levels run 1-20 (and beyond for some classes). It is a cleaned-up, more story-minded version of 1st Edition, still using descending AC and THAC0.

### Characters

- **Abilities:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. The default method is `3d6` six times in order, but offer `4d6kh3` six times (assigned freely) if the player wants a stronger party; both are in the book. Class minimums and race modifiers apply.
- **Races:** Human, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Half-elf, Halfling (half-orc in some supplements). Non-humans have class restrictions and level limits.
- **Classes in four groups:** Warriors (fighter, paladin, ranger), Wizards (mage and specialist wizards of the eight schools), Priests (cleric, druid, and specialty priests of a mythos), Rogues (thief, bard). Priests choose spheres of influence. Kits (such as Cavalier, Savage, Swashbuckler, Mercenary, Witch) customize a class. Alignment is the nine-point grid.
- **Non-weapon proficiencies** (a number by class and Intelligence) give each character skills, checked with a `1d20` roll at or under an ability score (modified). Weapon proficiencies are limited by class, with a penalty for non-proficiency.
- **Hit points:** fighter d10, ranger d10, paladin d10, cleric d8, druid d8, thief d6, bard d6, mage d4, with Constitution bonuses (warriors may get an exceptional bonus).
- **Armor Class is descending:** unarmored AC 10, leather 8, chain 5, plate 3; a shield improves it by 1; magic and Dexterity push it lower, into the negatives. **Lower is better.** State every AC in this convention.

### Play

- **THAC0:** To hit, roll `1d20` and succeed if the roll is at least THAC0 minus the target's AC. Every first-level character starts at THAC0 20. Warriors improve by 1 per level, priests by 2 every 3 levels, rogues by 1 every 2 levels, and wizards by 1 every 3 levels. Natural 20 hits and natural 1 misses.
- **Initiative:** each side rolls `1d10` (lower acts first). Weapon speed and casting times modify it, but run it as a plain side roll unless the story calls for the nuance.
- **Saving throws:** Paralyzation/Poison/Death Magic; Rod/Staff/Wand; Petrification/Polymorph; Breath Weapon; Spell. Roll `1d20`, meet or beat the class and level number.
- **Death:** 0 HP is death by default. If the table prefers a mercy rule, characters fall unconscious at 0 and die at -10, bleeding 1 HP per round. Raising the dead needs a cleric with the right spell and a Constitution check for the one raised.
- **Magic:** Vancian: wizards memorize spells by level from a spellbook; priests pray for spells by sphere. Spell schools and specialists give bonuses and restrictions. Casting a spell may be disrupted by a hit.
- **Exploration:** ten-minute dungeon turns, `1d6` for wandering monsters and listening, a morale roll `2d10` for monsters, and a reaction roll `2d10` for NPCs. Henchmen and followers come with the right level.
- **Advancement:** XP for monsters, treasure and class-specific actions (such as a thief's skill uses), and optional story awards. The "gold = XP" rule is no longer standard; story and goals matter more. Leveling happens automatically when the points are reached, with no training fee required (optional).
- **Danger:** still lethal, with a heavier emphasis on story, atmosphere, character kits and campaign settings. Let character quirks, backgrounds and ties to the world shape scenes.
