Record what kind of game the player wants. Session 0 only; the choice is final once the adventure begins.

`endless` is an open campaign with no planned story: whatever happens emerges from play. `short`, `medium` and `long` are stories with a beginning, middle and end (three chapters, a goal at the end, and more than one possible ending); they differ in how many quests the story takes and how big each quest is. A short story can be finished in a session or two.

Call this when the player has told you which they want, or when their own words already make it plain; calling it again with a different mode replaces the choice. `begin_adventure` is refused until this has been called, so ask the player if you do not know. Do not plan the story yourself: it is planned in secret after the adventure begins.
