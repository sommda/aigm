End the story. Call it once, at the climax of chapter 3, when the party's choices and the dice have decided how it ends: the final confrontation is resolved and the quests of chapter 3 are finished (report them with `set_scene` first).

- `ending`: the id of the ending in your story notes that best fits what happened, or `end_custom` if none of them does.
- `epilogue`: two or three sentences on what becomes of the world and the people the party met.
- `party_defeated`: true only if the whole party has fallen or the story can no longer go on; this lets you end the story outside chapter 3.

The result tells you to write the final scene. Do that in the same reply, then tell the player that the story is over and offer to keep playing in this world as an endless game.

When the notes say the story is lost, call this with the ending they name (or `end_custom` only for a failure the plan did not foresee), after you have finished the scene. You may not pick a kinder ending. The same goes in chapter 2 when one of the ways the game is lost, listed in your notes with its ending, has come to pass.
