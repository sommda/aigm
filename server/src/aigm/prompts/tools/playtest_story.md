Change the story's progress directly, in a story game. `chapter` jumps to chapter 1, 2 or 3 (quests of earlier chapters still open are skipped, and the goal becomes known); `quests` sets any quests to any status by id; `goal_revealed` sets whether the party knows the goal. No quest has to be active or earned. The next chapter's quests are planned in the background after a jump. This does not move the party or end the scene: do that yourself with `set_scene` and the other tools.
