Give party members experience points or levels. The server keeps each character's totals, tells the player, and shows every character's level and XP in your world notes, so call it whenever the party earns something instead of only describing it.

- `xp`: the experience points each of the named characters earns, in your ruleset's own scale, for a combat, a quest, a discovery, treasure or a clever solution. Work out the share yourself under your ruleset's rules (some divide the total among the party; some give each character the full amount).
- `who`: the characters who earn it. Leave it out for the whole party, which is the usual case. Name only some when the rest were absent, out of the fight, or the award is personal (a character's own goal, a class-specific award).
- `level_up`: one entry for each level a character has just gained, giving `who`, the `class` that gained it, that class's new `class_level`, and the character's new overall `level`. A level belongs to a class, and a character may have several, each at its own level. Your ruleset decides how the overall level follows from them (the total of the class levels, or the highest), so work it out yourself and pass it; the server only records it. Your ruleset also decides whether a character who levels up may take a level in a new or different class instead of their current one: offer that choice to the player when it applies, and record the class they pick. The first time you record a class for someone, give its level as it stands. How much XP a level takes depends on each character's class and the ruleset, so look up each one's threshold; do not assume the party levels together.
- `reason`: what earned it, in a few words.

After every award of XP, the result lists each character's total: compare each against the ruleset's advancement table for their class, and if anyone has earned a level, call `award` again with `level_up` for them, then update their sheets. Do this check every time. Call this in the same response as your other bookkeeping, before your final reply, and write no text beside it. The player sees the award; describe the new abilities in your reply when someone gains a level.
