Give the party experience points or a level. The server keeps the totals, tells the player, and shows the current level in your world notes, so call it whenever the party earns something instead of only describing it.

- `xp`: the experience points the whole party earned, in your ruleset's own scale, for a combat, a quest, a discovery, treasure or a clever solution. Use it when your ruleset advances by XP.
- `level`: the party's new level, exactly one above the current one. Use it the moment the total reaches the next level on your ruleset's table, or, if your ruleset levels by milestone, when a quest or story beat grants one. If a level is gained, update every character sheet in your reply.
- `reason`: what earned it, in a few words.

You can give `xp` and `level` together. Call this in the same response as your other bookkeeping, before your final reply, and write no text beside it. The player sees the award; describe the new abilities in your reply when a level is gained.
