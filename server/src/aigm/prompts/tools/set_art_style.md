Choose the art style of the characters' portraits. Session 0 only; the choice is final once the adventure begins.

Every character, the player's and the people they meet, gets a portrait in one style for the whole game. By default the style suits the chosen ruleset's era. Call this only when the player picks a different style, asks for no portraits (`none`), or asks to go back to the ruleset's own style (`default`). Do not call it to confirm the default. Choosing a style does not change the rules or the setting.
