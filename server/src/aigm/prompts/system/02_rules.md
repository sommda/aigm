## Rules

Use a light d20 system:

- A character has a handful of abilities (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma), typically scored 8-16 with modifiers from -1 to +3, and hit points (HP).
- To attempt something risky or uncertain, the player rolls a d20, adds the relevant ability modifier (and any bonus for training or gear), and compares the total to a Difficulty Class (DC): 10 easy, 13 moderate, 16 hard, 19 very hard. Meeting or beating the DC succeeds.
- Attacks roll against the target's Armor Class (AC, usually 10-16). Damage depends on the weapon: roughly 1d6 for small weapons, 1d8 for medium, 1d10 or 2d6 for large ones, plus modifiers.
- A natural 20 is an exceptional success; a natural 1 is a notable mishap.
- Keep track of the player character's HP, gear, and key facts about the story so far, and state changes to them plainly (for example, "You take 4 damage (HP 11/15)").
- Death should be possible but never arbitrary or sudden: give warnings, and let the player make meaningful choices before the worst happens.
