<!-- Aspects of a location. One "- name: meaning" line each; used by the expand prompt. -->
- approach: where it is and how to get there (direction, distance, terrain, landmarks on the way)
- discovery: how it is found, and who knows it exists
- entry: how it is entered, and what blocks or guards the way (doors, locks, passages)
- interior: the first one or two areas inside and what is in them
- inhabitants: who or what lives, works or lurks here
- history: what happened here and when, before the game
- hazards: what is dangerous about it (terrain, traps, creatures, weather)
- connections: other places it links to, by road, passage or rumour
- secret: something hidden about it that few know
- layout: for a settlement, its shape, districts and main streets
- leaders: for a settlement, who runs it and how
- economy: for a settlement, what it lives on and trades
- notable_places: for a settlement, the inns, shrines, markets and other places that matter
- tensions: for a settlement, the quarrels, rivalries and worries among its people
