<!-- Aspects of a person. -->
- role: who they are and what they do
- appearance: what a stranger would notice
- manner: voice, habits, how they treat people
- motivation: what they want, and what they fear
- relationships: who they are tied to (family, allies, rivals, debts)
- knowledge: what they know and would tell, and what they would not
- location: where they usually are and their daily routine
